Phase transition criteria
Phase 1 → Transition:
✓ Oedema resolving (now at + level)
✓ Medical complications improving
✓ Child taking most F-75 feeds orally
Transition: Give F-100 at same volume as F-75 for 1–2 days, increase by 10ml each feed
Transition → Phase 2 (Rehabilitation):
✓ Good appetite — taking ≥75% of F-100 prescribed
✓ Oedema at + level or fully resolved
✓ Medical problems resolved
Phase 2: Free feeding with F-100 (150–220 kcal/kg/day), introduce RUTF
Return to Phase 1 (any one of these):
✗ Increasing or new oedema
✗ Rapid liver enlargement or abdominal distension
✗ Signs of fluid overload
✗ Weight loss from refeeding diarrhoea
✗ Weight loss 2 consecutive days
✗ Static weight 3 consecutive days
✗ Any severe complication (hypoglycaemia, shock, hypothermia)
Transfer to OTC (all must be met):
✓ No danger signs for 24–48 hrs
✓ Medical problems resolved
✓ No ongoing weight loss
✓ Oedema not +++ and resolving
✓ Good appetite (≥75% RUTF prescribed)
✓ Caregiver willing and able
